# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Job applicants across a broad spectrum: experienced trade professionals (HVAC technicians, plumbers, electricians with Gas Safe/F-Gas certifications), mechanical and electrical engineers, office and administrative staff, and entry-level candidates seeking apprenticeships or their first roles in the industry. Users are accessing from desktops and mobile devices, often during job search sessions, and expect a frictionless, trustworthy process that respects their time.

## Product Purpose

The CV application page enables candidates to submit their contact details and CV to NTS Ltd in a single, professional interaction. The goal is to create a seamless first impression—one that signals NTS takes hiring seriously, values candidate experience, and maintains the same standards of professionalism and technical competence that define the company's engineering work. Success means candidates complete the application without friction, feel confident their submission was received, and come away impressed by NTS's attention to detail.

## Brand Personality

Professional, confident, technical, approachable. NTS is a company with 45+ years of engineering expertise and Gas Safe credentials—that credibility is non-negotiable. But hiring should feel human and supportive, not gatekeeping. The voice is clear and direct (like good engineering documentation), but warm enough that candidates at all experience levels feel welcomed.

**3-word personality:** Skilled, Trustworthy, Accessible

## Anti-references

- Corporate job boards that feel generic, impersonal, or slow (Indeed, LinkedIn's application funnel)
- Overly casual or playful hiring pages that undermine credibility (startup culture landing pages that treat serious roles flippantly)
- Dense forms with unnecessary fields or confusing instructions
- Form design that looks unfinished, unresponsive, or abandoned
- Error states that shame or confuse users rather than guide them

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over decoration** — Every field, instruction, and button serves a purpose. No busy backgrounds, no decorative text. The form is the star, not chrome.
2. **Confidence through consistency** — Use the established NTS brand system (navy, green, dark backgrounds, professional typography). Consistent patterns signal this is the real NTS, not a phishing clone.
3. **Accessibility is professionalism** — Clear labels, readable contrast, keyboard navigation, and mobile-first responsiveness prove NTS respects all candidates equally. No special skills required to apply.
4. **Progress and feedback** — Candidates always know what's happening: required fields are obvious, upload success is instant, errors are specific and fixable, submission confirmation is unambiguous.
5. **One job, one commitment** — The form is focused. No distractions, no "while you're here, check out our other pages." The candidate's job is to apply; our job is to make that easy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum (WCAG AAA for color contrast and interactive elements where feasible)
- Keyboard-accessible forms (Tab order, Enter to submit, Escape to clear focus)
- No reliance on color alone for meaning (error states use icons + text, upload states use text + visual feedback)
- Mobile-first and responsive: tested at 375px (mobile), 768px (tablet), 1280px (desktop)
- Form validation that is specific and helpful, not punitive
- File upload feedback that works for all users (not just those hovering)
- Clear language, no jargon specific to tech or HR
