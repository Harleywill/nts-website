Google Reviews / TrustPilot Integration Options

Where and how should reviews appear on your website?

A

Replace Current Testimonials

Replace your manual testimonials carousel with live Google/TrustPilot reviews. Real reviews pull directly from the platform.

Best for: Maximum social proof, auto-updating
Tradeoff: Less control over narrative, dependent on review availability
B

Hybrid: Add to Current Section

Keep your testimonials carousel, add a "Latest Reviews" section below showing live Google/TrustPilot reviews. Best of both worlds.

Best for: Balanced approach, curated + real reviews
Tradeoff: More content on page, requires space
C

Dedicated Reviews Page

Create a new /reviews page showcasing Google/TrustPilot reviews with ratings, distribution charts, and filters. Link from homepage.

Best for: Comprehensive review showcase, detailed analytics
Tradeoff: Requires separate page, less integrated with homepage
D

Footer Badge / Trust Signals

Add Google/TrustPilot certification badges in footer with average rating. Links to full reviews. Minimal visual footprint.

Best for: Subtle trust building, footer real estate
Tradeoff: Less prominent, minimal review visibility
